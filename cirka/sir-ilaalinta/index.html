Cirday waa app muujiya cirka habeenkii sida looga arko dhulka Soomaalida: xiddigaha, meerayaasha, dayaxa, godadka iyo majarayaalka. Boggani wuxuu sharxayaa xogta app-ku isticmaalo iyo sida loo ilaaliyo.

Xogta aan uruurinno

Cirday kuma laha akoon, ma uruuriyo magacaaga ama email-kaaga, mana isticmaalo xayeysiis ama barnaamijyo la socda isticmaalka (analytics). Wax xog ah oo shakhsi ah lama keydiyo server-kayaga.

Goobta (GPS)

Haddii aad oggolaato, app-ku wuxuu isticmaalaa goobtaada si uu u xisaabiyo cirka sida uu meeshaas uga muuqdo: waqtiyada soo bixidda iyo dhicidda, godka dayaxa iyo muuqaalka dhulka ee jiifka. Goobta waxaa lagu keydiyaa taleefankaaga oo keliya (kaydka browser-ka), lagumana diro server-kayaga. Goob gacanta ayaad u dooran kartaa, GPS la'aan.

Kaamirada iyo dareemayaasha taleefanka

Marka aad taleefanka cirka u jeediso, app-ku wuxuu isticmaalaa dareemayaasha jihada (compass iyo gyroscope). Haddii aad shido muuqaalka kaamirada, sawirka kaamirada waxaa lagu muujiyaa shaashadda oo keliya: lama duubo, lama keydiyo, meelna looma diro.

Adeegyada dibadda

Si app-ku u shaqeeyo, wuxuu xog dadweyne ah ka soo dejiyaa adeegyadan. Marka sidaas la sameeyo, adeeggu wuxuu arki karaa cinwaanka IP-ga taleefankaaga, sida bog kasta oo internet ah:

  • Terrain Tiles (Amazon Web Services): xogta joogga dhulka ee goobtaada agagaarkeeda, si buuraha jiifka loo sawiro. Waxaa la codsadaa qaybo khariidad ah oo qiyaastii 70 km ah, ma aha goobtaada saxda ah.
  • Open-Meteo: marka aad magac magaalo raadiso, magacaas ayaa loo diraa si goobta loo helo.
  • CelesTrak: wareegyada dayax-gacmeedyada (sida ISS).
  • Google Fonts: farta qoraalka.

Privacy Policy

Cirday app · Last updated 25 November 2026

Cirday is an app that shows the night sky as seen from the Somali lands: stars, planets, the Moon, the lunar mansions (godad) and the star compass. This page explains what data the app uses and how it is protected.

Data we collect

Cirday has no accounts, does not collect your name or email, and uses no advertising or analytics tools. No personal data is stored on our servers.

Location (GPS)

If you allow it, the app uses your location to compute the sky as seen from where you are: rise and set times, the Moon's mansion and the shape of the local horizon. Your location is stored only on your device (browser storage) and is never sent to our servers. You can choose a place manually instead of using GPS.

Camera and device sensors

When you point your phone at the sky, the app uses the orientation sensors (compass and gyroscope). If you turn on the camera view, the camera image is only shown on screen: it is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Third-party services

To work, the app downloads public data from these services. As with any web page, the service can see your device's IP address:

  • Terrain Tiles (Amazon Web Services): elevation data around your location, used to draw the hills on the horizon. Map tiles covering roughly 70 km are requested, not your exact position.
  • Open-Meteo: when you search for a town by name, that name is sent to find its location.
  • CelesTrak: satellite orbits (such as the ISS).
  • Google Fonts: the text fonts.

On-device storage

Your settings (language, layers, location, favourite stars) and the local terrain data are stored on your device so the app can work offline. You can delete them by uninstalling the app or clearing the browser storage.

Children

Cirday is an educational app suitable for all ages. No personal data is collected from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the way data is handled changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
